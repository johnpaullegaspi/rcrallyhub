*Placeholder policy — update deposit amounts, cutoff windows, and refund percentages once finalized.*

Reservations may be cancelled by contacting RC Rally Hub through phone, email, or Facebook Messenger at least **24 hours** before the scheduled start time to be eligible for a full refund of any deposit paid.

Cancellations made **less than 24 hours** before the scheduled start time may forfeit part or all of the deposit, at management's discretion.

**No-shows** (failing to arrive within 15 minutes of the scheduled start time without prior notice) are treated as cancellations and are not eligible for a refund.

Event and tournament registrations follow the specific cancellation terms listed on each event's detail page, which may differ from standard court-rental cancellations.
