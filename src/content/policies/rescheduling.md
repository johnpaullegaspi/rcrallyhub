*Placeholder policy — update cutoff windows and limits once finalized.*

Need to change your date or time instead of cancelling? Reach out at least **12 hours** before your scheduled session and we'll do our best to move your reservation to another available slot, subject to court availability.

Rescheduled bookings retain their original deposit — no additional payment is required unless the new slot falls under a different rate (for example, moving from an off-peak to a peak-hour slot).

Reservations may be rescheduled up to **two times** per original booking. Additional changes will be treated as a new reservation.
